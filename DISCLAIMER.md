# Disclaimer

ProofStamp via Solana is experimental open-source software. The current application uses **Solana devnet** and is not a production service.

## What a ProofStamp can show

A ProofStamp can provide evidence that a particular SHA-256 file digest was recorded in a public Solana transaction at a particular blockchain-reported time.

## What it does not prove

A ProofStamp does not, by itself, prove:

- who created, owned, sent, or received the file,
- when the underlying content was originally created or captured,
- whether the content is true, authentic, accurate, lawful, or unaltered before stamping,
- delivery, receipt, acceptance, or agreement by another party,
- that a file is legally notarized, legally signed, admissible evidence, or sufficient for any regulatory requirement.

The proof concerns specific file bytes and the public record of their SHA-256 digest.

## Devnet limitations

Solana devnet is a test network. Devnet infrastructure, accounts, transactions, RPC endpoints, explorers, and historical data may change, reset, fail, or become unavailable. Devnet records should not be relied on for production, legal, financial, compliance, or archival purposes.
